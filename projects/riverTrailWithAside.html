Lawrence River Trail

Meandering along theKaw River, the Rival Trail is a wooded ten-mile single-lane loop located in North Lawrence. It's rated easy, but there are plenty of tight turns, rocks, logs, and poisonous snakes to you on your toes.

Directions:

On the north side of the Mass Street Bridge, go east on Elm Street. Turn right on 8th street, look for the trailhead, and park.

Trail Rules:

  1. Only ride when the trail is open.
  2. Riders go clock; hikers go other direction.
  3. Hikers yield to riders.
  4. Discard trash discreetly under available brush.
  5. Don't feed the snakes.
