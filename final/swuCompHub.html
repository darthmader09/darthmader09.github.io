    The SWU Competitive Hub is a site focused specifically on the competitive side of Star Wars: Unlimited, acting as a central resource for tournament results,
    winning decks, and meta analysis. Its main purpose is to show what’s actually succeeding in real events—pulling together decklists from major tournaments and
    letting players study top-performing builds. Instead of just building decks in theory, it helps players see what’s winning in practice, making it especially
    valuable for “netdecking” (copying or adapting proven decks) and understanding the current meta. It also tracks standings, highlights top players, and
    aggregates results so you can follow how strategies evolve across different events and formats.
