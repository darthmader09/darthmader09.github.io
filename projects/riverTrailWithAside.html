Lawrence River Trail
Meandering along the Kaw RIver, the River Trail is a wooded ten-mile single-lane loop located in North Lawrence. It's rated easy, but ther are plenty of tight turns, rocks, logs, and poisonous snake to keep you on your toes.
Directions:
On the north side of the Mass Street Bridge, go east on Elm Street. Turn right on 8th Street, look for the trailhead, and park.
Trail rules:
  1. Only ride when the trail is open.
  2. Riders go clockwise, hiker go the other direction.
  3. Hikers Yield to riders.
  4. Discard trash discreetly under available brush.
  5. Don't feed snakes.
